# The AI Kids Games name and logo

The code and the games in this repository are open source under the [MIT License](LICENSE). You
can fork them, change them and publish your own versions.

The **AI Kids Games** name and logo are different: they identify the official website, at
https://ai-kids-games-fawn.vercel.app, and the games the project publishes there. They are not covered by the MIT License. The
logo is in [docs/brand](docs/brand).

## Please do

- Say your project is "built with" or "based on AI Kids Games", with a link to this repository.
- Use the name in plain text to talk about the project, for example in an article or a review.
- Keep the copyright and license notices that come with the code.

## Please don't

- Publish a fork, a copy of the website or a game under the AI Kids Games name or logo, or a name
  or address that could be mistaken for it.
- Suggest that the project endorses your site, game or product.
- Use the logo in your own app icon, store listing or advertising.

## Forks and your own games

Give your fork or game its **own name, logo and look**. The games' own titles and characters
(TheaBonk, Light Crossing, Vanex Defense, Space Sky, Calex Cart and Cliff Valley) belong to the
project too, so if you publish a changed version of a game, rename it.

Want your game on the AI Kids Games website itself? See *List your game* in the README.

Questions: open a Discussion.
