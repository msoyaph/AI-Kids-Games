# Plan 2: finish the farm game and expand its mechanics

Owner brief, 2026-10-14: "Finish the farm game end to end, and expand its mechanics". The style frame
is approved. This plan covers four things:

- an audit of what is built;
- the design for the ten new mechanics;
- the phases, pacing and save sizes;
- five web-checked names.

Work starts on the owner's "go", which also picks the name.

The original plan (`docs/PLAN.md`) and every rule in `CLAUDE.md` still apply:

- different from Cliff Valley;
- no energy, real-world timers, decay, purchases or real people;
- all art and audio made in code;
- the inspiration used as a quality reference only (IP log);
- the quiet check before any heavy run;
- full browser suites run against previews, never against production.

**Release:** stop before production. Open the PR, get CI green and hand over the preview link. This
replaces the earlier "deploy when complete".

## 2. Design: the expanded mechanics

Every new system is pure sim code (seeded and testable), runs through one action dispatcher shared
by the UI, the helpers, the bot and the tests (`sim/actions.ts`), and is kind by construction. Every
system follows the same five rules:

- nothing is ever lost or ruined;
- no deadline is missed;
- there are no wages and no tiredness;
- a mistake only means a little less;
- nothing can be bought with real money.

### 2.1 Helpers: the automation and planning layer
- **Who they are:** Larkford volunteers, the farmhands already walking the farm. They are original
  characters, with names web-checked and logged.
- **The panel:** reached from the cottage or the Farm Book. You assign each helper a job and, where it
  fits, a zone or a building:
  - **Watering:** a zone you drag out; they water dry growing fields in it.
  - **Harvesting:** a zone; ready crops and fruit go to the barn.
  - **Animals:** chosen homes; they feed from the barn's feed and collect products.
  - **Workshop:** one workshop and a recipe; they keep its queue filled while ingredients last.
  - **Deliveries:** they load the boat for board orders and contract drops, and restock the farm stand
    from the barn up to the shelf levels you set.
- **Work rate:** one action every 6 s of play at skill 1, falling to 3 s at skill 5.
- **Skills:** each helper has 5 skills (one per job) that grow with use; levels come at 20, 60, 140
  and 300 actions. A level-up shows a cheerful toast.
- **Never:** wages, tiredness, mistakes or penalties. An idle helper rests on the cottage bench.
- **Hiring** (no coins):
  - 1 helper in chapter 3 (the first);
  - +1 at level 9, at the end of chapter 6, and at level 16;
  - up to 2 more from Lab nodes;
  - 6 at most.
- **On screen:** the farmhands mirror the sim. They walk to the tile of each action and animate it.
  Their reactions to the player's own work stay as they are.

### 2.2 Soil health
- **Two nutrients per field,** each shown as 0 to 3 dots:
  - **Nitrogen** (leaf icon): what plants build leaves from.
  - **Humus** (worm icon): organic matter that holds water and feeds soil life.
- **What changes them:**
  - Heavy feeders (grains, squash, cabbage family, garden crops) use 1 nitrogen per harvest.
  - Legumes add 1 nitrogen.
  - Clover as a cover crop adds 2 nitrogen and 1 humus. It's quick, and its harvest is optional.
  - Compost adds 1 humus (and still speeds growth by 25%).
  - Rotating to a different family keeps humus, while repeating a family uses 1.
  - A field left empty for a season regains 1 humus.
- **Effect on quality:** nitrogen ≥ 2 gives +1 care point, and humus ≥ 2 gives +1 care point. This
  replaces today's one-off "nitrogen boost".
- **Never ruined:** at 0 a field simply gives no bonus, and it never drops below the old baseline.
- **Where it shows:** soil dots on the field card, a soil overlay in Seeds mode, and "Soil science"
  pages in the Crop Book. Old saves start at nitrogen 2 and humus 1.

### 2.3 The farm stand
- **The stall:** your own stall on the Larkford market square.
- **Stocking it:** 6 slots (8 and 10 through the Lab), stocked from the barn. Items keep forever; take
  them back any time.
- **Prices:** a slider per slot from 60% to 160% of the market price. A friendly label reads "Bargain",
  "Fair", "A bit pricey" or "Pricey".
- **Shoppers:** one arrives about every 25 s of play while the farm runs. Each considers every slot:
  `P(buy) = base(item, season) × (1.8 − price ratio)^1.4 × (1 + 0.08 × distinct items)`, clamped to
  0..0.9. They buy 1 to 2.
  - Seasonal demand favours, for example, lemonade in summer and soup in winter.
  - The variety bonus rewards a varied stall.
- **The lesson:** a "Today at the stand" card and a Ledger page show sales against price, with one
  plain tip ("Strawberries sold out fast at 'Fair'. You could try a little higher."). The worst a
  mistake does is sell a bit less.
- **When it opens:** chapter 2. Shoppers walk the 3D town square.

### 2.4 Contracts
- **Four businesses:**
  - the bakery: Tomasz, from the existing cast;
  - the school lunch kitchen: Amara, from the existing cast;
  - the café and the fabric shop: two new owners, names web-checked.
- **A contract** is 3 to 5 drops of a set basket, for example 6 flour and 4 eggs. The next drop is
  "wanted" about once a day of play but never expires. It waits as long as needed, with no penalty.
- **Delivery:** drops go by boat, or a helper on Deliveries does it.
- **Rewards:** each drop pays more than the board (×1.7). Finishing pays a bonus of coins, 2 Lab
  Ideas, a story moment and an unlock, such as a recipe, a decoration set or a helper.
- **Pacing:** one contract per business at a time, unlocked by chapters 3, 5, 6 and 7, then repeating
  with new baskets after the story.

### 2.5 Greenhouses and irrigation
- **Greenhouse:** a building. Small is 2×2 with 4 inside plots; medium (3×3, 8 plots) and large (4×3,
  12 plots) come from the Lab.
  - Any crop grows in any season at full speed. That makes for a cosy winter.
  - You plant and pick through its card, or a helper does.
  - Inside it is drawn with glass, plants on benches and a warm glow at night.
- **Irrigation channels:** painted in build mode like paths (stored in the same per-tile string).
  - A channel network is "wet" when any tile of it touches the river or a pond.
  - Fields beside a wet channel are watered at dawn and when planted.
  - A dry network shows a dashed outline and a hint.
- **Sprinklers:** 1×1 decor. They work only on or beside a wet channel, so planning the layout is the
  puzzle. They water fields within radius 1 (2 and 3 through the Lab) at dawn and when planted.
- **When it unlocks:** chapter 5 for water, chapter 6 for greenhouses.

### 2.6 The Farm Lab
- **The tree:** about 24 nodes in 6 branches:
  - **Tools:** watering drag covers 2 rows; a bigger barn discount.
  - **Workshops:** +1 queue slot, 10% faster.
  - **Water:** sprinkler radius 2 and 3; channels carry through paths.
  - **Glass:** medium and large greenhouses; a winter growth bonus.
  - **Helpers:** faster skill growth; +1 and +1 helper.
  - **Decor:** 3 new decoration sets.
- **Ideas,** the currency of the Lab, are earned only:
  - +1 per level from level 3;
  - +2 per finished contract;
  - +1 per fair ribbon in the top 3;
  - +1 per new variety discovered.
- **Never** for sale and never bought with coins. Opens in chapter 6 (the cottage study).

### 2.7 Seed cross-breeding
- **The crops:** 12 flowering crops each get 1 heirloom variety, sold from chapter 4, and 1 to 2
  varieties waiting to be discovered. All are real colour or flavour variants, such as lemon cucumber,
  white pumpkin, red onion, yellow watermelon, purple bell pepper and golden zucchini.
- **Discovery:** a field of a flowering crop is harvested while a 4-neighbour field holds the same crop
  of a different variety, with a hive within 5 tiles. Each such harvest has a seeded 20% chance (25%
  with healthy soil) to discover the cross. A celebration card names it.
- **What you get:** varieties get their own seeds, a slight price bonus and +1 fair score, and they
  enter the "Best new variety" category.
- **Crop Book:** a short, real explanation. Bees carry pollen between flowers, seeds from crossed
  flowers mix their parents' traits, and growers keep the best over many seasons.

### 2.8 Gentle weather events
- **The forecast:** the date pill shows the next 2 days, and tapping it opens a forecast card.
  Everything is announced at least a day ahead, at most one event a day, seeded.
- **The events:**
  - **Rain:** waters every field.
  - **Sunny spell:** +15% growth that day.
  - **Windy day:** tap "Tie down the hay" on the card beforehand for a small bonus: happier animals
    and a little XP.
  - **Misty morning:** mushrooms to forage at the woods' edge.
  - **First snow:** greenhouses cosy, +XP for winter crafting.
  - **Warm summer night:** fireflies.
- **Only bonuses:** preparing gives a small bonus; not preparing loses nothing.
- **On screen:** rain, snow, gusts and fireflies, plus the read-aloud forecast.

### 2.9 Harvest Fair categories and the ribbon wall
- **When:** a fair at each season's end, from chapter 5; the Grand Harvest Fair ends chapter 8.
- **Four categories:**
  - **Biggest produce:** your best crop. Its stars plus a "giant" chance from 3 stars and healthy soil.
  - **Best-made goods:** stars and recipe depth.
  - **Prettiest farm:** judged on the beauty rating, no entry needed.
  - **Best new variety:** bred varieties only.
- **The neighbours** enter with seeded scores that rise over the year.
- **The prizes:** ribbons (gold, silver, bronze and "thank you for coming") hang on a Ribbon Wall panel,
  and a ribbon-wall decoration appears in the cottage yard. Prizes are coins, Lab Ideas and
  decoration sets.

### 2.10 Seasons and night
- **All four seasons** in the approved look, including:
  - snow on roofs and ground in winter (a shader term, with no extra models);
  - blossom drift in spring;
  - leaves in autumn;
  - frost-rimmed water in winter.
- **Night:** lit windows that bloom, lanterns, fireflies, and the stars in the sky dome.
- **A cozy winter:** few field crops grow, so it centres on workshop crafting, greenhouse growing and
  the Lab. Chapter 6 is set in winter.

### 2.11 How they tie together
Orders, contracts and the stand are three ways to sell, from quick to planned to priced.

- Helpers, irrigation and greenhouses are how a bigger farm keeps up.
- Soil and breeding are how quality grows.
- The Lab and the fairs reward all of it.

Each chapter's goals use the systems it unlocks, so the story teaches them one at a time.

### The eight chapters
The target is 6 to 8 hours of play. One season is 48 minutes, and the story runs from late winter of
year 1 to autumn of year 2. The bot checks every chapter, and the pacing test fails outside 6 to 8
hours.

| Ch | Name | Season | Introduces | About |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | The Gate Key | late winter to spring | clearing, fields, board and boat | 35 min |
| 2 | Eggs and Elbow Grease | spring | coop, compost, soil, the farm stand | 45 min |
| 3 | The Baker's Bargain | late spring | mill and bakery, the first helper, the bakery contract, the Meadow | 50 min |
| 4 | Busy Bees | summer | hives, orchards, heirlooms and breeding, the forecast, neighbours | 50 min |
| 5 | Water Wise | autumn | channels and sprinklers, Orchard Hill, the school contract, the first fair | 55 min |
| 6 | Glass and Wool | winter | greenhouse, Farm Lab, loom and kitchen, the café contract, the second helper | 55 min |
| 7 | River Days | spring, year 2 | Pond and Riverside, ducks, the fabric-shop contract, co-op orders | 60 min |
| 8 | The Grand Harvest Fair | summer to autumn, year 2 | Hilltop and the four-category Grand Fair, then credits and endless goals | 60 min |

**Balance:**
- Later crops get rebalanced to pay at least as well per field-minute as early ones.
- The simulation checks that no single crop, product or price strategy beats the mix by more than 15%.
- It checks that every chapter is reachable without grinding (a chapter waits at most 10 minutes on
  coins).
- It checks recovery from 0 coins in every chapter.

## 4. Save size

Measured from today's JSON shapes:

- **Worst case:** about 430 KB, with every dry tile a growing field.
- **A heavy late-game farm:** about 150 to 170 KB.
- **The problem:** the site's origin already holds about 3.7 MB of its 5 MB limit at the caps, so
  430 KB (or 860 KB with a backup copy) is too much.

**Save v2** (phase 14) fixes this:

- objects become compact tuples per kind, with times rounded to 0.1 s and short keys;
- soil is 2 characters a field;
- channels share the per-tile string with paths;
- the ledger keeps 8 compact pages, and stats are not copied.

| | v2 estimate |
|---|---|
| Worst case (1,607 tiles all used, every system in use) | ≈ 120 KB |
| With one rolling backup copy (`<prefix>save.bak`, written every 5 minutes of play) | ≈ 240 KB |
| Heavy normal play | ≈ 50 KB |

A unit test builds a maximum farm with every system in use and fails above 140 KB a copy.

A failed write shows a friendly "Your browser's storage is full" card and keeps playing. Version-1
saves migrate, with fixtures.
