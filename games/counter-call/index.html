Friends are hungry! Cook, carry and serve.

This browser can't save progress, so your stars last until you close the game.

How to play

  1. Look at the bubble over a friend to see what they want.
  2. Tap a station to start cooking. It dings when the food is ready.
  3. Tap the station again to put the food on your tray.
  4. Tap the friend to serve them before their bar runs out.

You can line up four taps in a row. Bring a whole order at once, and keep friends happy in a row, for bonus coins.

Ring the bell to call the next friend in sooner. Tap the cake stand to share free cake when friends get restless. Tap food on the tray to put it back.

Keyboard: 1 to 5 for stations, Q W E for friends, B for the bell, C for cake, Backspace to put food back, P to pause.
