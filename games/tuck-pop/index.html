How to play

  1. Drag a piece from the tray onto the board. Or tap a piece, then tap the board.
  2. Fill a whole row or column and it pops. Clear several at once for more points.
  3. Keep clearing move after move to build a streak and multiply your points.
  4. Pieces don't turn. The game ends when none of your pieces fit.
  5. Keyboard: 1, 2, 3 pick a piece, arrows move it, Enter places it, Escape puts it back, U undoes.
  6. Gamepad: LB and RB pick, the d-pad moves, A places, B puts it back, X undoes, Start pauses.
